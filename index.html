Source Segregation Streams

The foundation of circular waste management lies in clear distinction between Wet (Biodegradable), Dry (Recyclable), and Domestic Hazardous waste streams.

Green Bin

Wet & Biodegradable Waste

Organic materials capable of microbial decomposition into nutrient-dense compost.

Included Items

Fruit & vegetable peels, leftover food, tea leaves, eggshells, garden leaves, fallen flowers.

Strict Exclusions

Plastic packaging, aluminium foils, glass bottles, diapers, sanitary pads.

Converts to organic compost & biogas
Blue Bin

Dry Recyclable Waste

Clean, non-biodegradable materials destined for mechanical recycling and repurposing.

Included Items

Rinsed plastic bottles, cardboard cartons, newspapers, clean glass bottles, metal soda cans.

Strict Exclusions

Food-stained oily pizza boxes, thermocol/styrofoam, medical syringes, wet tissue paper.

Saved from incinerators & landfills
Red / Black Bin

Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste

Toxic, corrosive, flammable, or chemical elements requiring specialized treatment facilities.

Included Items

Lithium batteries, button cells, mercury thermometers, CFL tubes, pesticide bottles, discarded medicines.

Disposal Rule

Never mix with general garbage. Always wrap terminals with tape and hand over to certified e-waste recyclers.

Protects workers from toxins & fires
